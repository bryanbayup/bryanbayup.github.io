A little about me

From keeping systems running to teaching machines to learn.

My background started in IT infrastructure and networking. I worked with routers, switches, servers, field teams, and the everyday problems that come with keeping technical operations running smoothly.

Along the way, I became increasingly interested in data, machine learning, and AI. That interest led me to take part in structured programs such as Bangkit and Coding Camp powered by DBS Foundation, where I worked on practical machine-learning projects rather than learning only from theory.

Today, I see infrastructure and AI as two parts of the same engineering mindset: understand the system, identify the real problem, build something that works, and keep learning when the first solution isn't enough.
